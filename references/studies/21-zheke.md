## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed display is essential to the square layout; heavier wide sans handles short lead-ins. The palette and repeated corner lead-in keep short cards coherent. The ending uses extreme size contrast between the name and numerical line.

## Composition and spatial structure

The square frame is filled by stacked words, while the upper-left lead-in remains fixed during demonstrations. Material and particle examples stay inside the same broad text hierarchy, reducing the feeling of an unrelated demo reel.

## Colour, gradients and material

Black, cream, orange and cobalt dominate. Glossy spheres carry genuine highlight gradients and warm shading; simpler type cards use solid fields. The lens/cloud sequence deliberately introduces a short, softer material passage.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show bouncing dot continuity across palette cuts, per-letter drop-ins, a perspective type ring, a swarm release and abrupt callback cuts. The isometric build uses a visible base plane; the tunnel uses nested silhouettes and changing scale. A lens effect requires a displacement/refractive treatment, not only lowered opacity.

## Pace and density

Capability cards are short and intensify into a rapid reprise near ten seconds. The final statement and lockup slow down enough to read. The opening establishes the framing before the fastest montage starts.
