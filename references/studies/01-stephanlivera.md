## Typography and hierarchy

A very heavy wide uppercase grotesk supplies the hero voice; an italic high-contrast serif supplies the role subtitle; tiny mono-like labels remain peripheral. The hierarchy is created by width and mass, not many font families. Cropped rapid words read as shapes, while the final mark is fully readable. Exact font identities are unverified.

## Composition and spatial structure

Most hero objects stay on the central axis, with generous empty margins and persistent corner registration. Full-bleed pattern sections deliberately break that quiet framing. Thin rules, six aligned demonstration rows and the footer baseline share an editorial grid.

## Colour, gradients and material

Ink and warm cream establish contrast; coral is the brand anchor, cobalt the contrasting chapter, and acid yellow a short typographic accent. Most fields are flat. Soft colour fringes occur near fast enlargements; do not misclassify motion blur or compression as a permanent gradient treatment.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Expansion, shape replacement, orbit, tessellation deformation, point-cloud projection and kinetic text have distinct motion signatures. The transition windows show scale-through masks, a diagonal wipe, hard palette changes and directional smearing. A transferable implementation is to map a seed into a ring, then into a full-screen matte, retaining the centre and accent.

## Pace and density

The longest explanatory section is the easing comparison; the fastest text montage occupies roughly two seconds near the climax. The outro gives about 1.5 seconds of resolved branding. Density and velocity increase independently, so the peak is not merely a longer list of scenes.
