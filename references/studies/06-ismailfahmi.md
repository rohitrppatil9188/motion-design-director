## Typography and hierarchy

Rounded heavy title type is secondary to the particle story until the ending. Captions use a clean small sans with strong white/navy contrast. The title's colour sweep is a deliberate gradient, while supporting text stays mostly neutral.

## Composition and spatial structure

The hero volume is centred above a persistent bottom-caption zone. Top-right figures provide a secondary anchor. Unlike rapid showreel montages, the camera remains comparatively stable, letting topology changes carry the story.

## Colour, gradients and material

Deep navy and a broad subtle blue illumination field establish depth. White particles become cyan, cream/yellow and pink clusters. Hubs are brightest; connectors are dimmer. Value hierarchy matters more than adding glow to every element.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native-frame windows show mostly continuous deformation rather than wholesale cuts. Particle directions stretch into short strokes during faster passages. A reusable implementation uses a fixed seeded point index, several target layouts and smooth position blending, then depth-dependent stroke length and opacity. The displayed network and numerical claims are illustrative source content, not verified real data.

## Pace and density

Longer 3–4-second topology passages support a narrative proposition. The peak chart and final title receive meaningful holds. It demonstrates that a polished 15-second reel can use one evolving system rather than a new effect every half-second.
