## Typography and hierarchy

Very wide bold sans gives the title cards impact; outline duplicates remain low contrast. The yellow scene's italic serif provides a late tonal change. The final role line repeats the serif without competing with the name.

## Composition and spatial structure

The central axis is shared by line, shape construction and tunnel. Fine perimeter furniture and a right-hand graph make the shape section feel designed. The yellow card uses a clear text-left/art-right balance.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, electric blue, cream, pale violet and yellow form separated fields. Richness comes from pattern and line work more than soft gradients. Blue illumination is concentrated in the tunnel.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Five-frame windows reveal line expansion at 1.13s, a shape-to-cell-field reveal near 5.85s, diagonal occlusion near 7.7s and yellow block wipe near 11.43s. Tunnel geometry should use consistent camera/projective math and a deliberate roll, not arbitrary per-layer rotation.

## Pace and density

The dense tile pattern and tunnel each sustain a recognisable passage, while early word cards move faster. A calm yellow card precedes the final dark identity, creating a useful deceleration.
