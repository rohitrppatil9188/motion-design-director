## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed display expresses the main proposition; handwritten annotations make documents feel tactile and explained. The final name uses a clean sans with a restrained serif support line, distinct from the earlier demonstration type.

## Composition and spatial structure

The ball remains central as documents gather around it. White space, paper shadows and annotations create a coherent physical workspace. The graph has clear axes and a direct comparison rather than an overloaded dashboard.

## Colour, gradients and material

Teal, warm white, dark green and small muted red/brown annotation accents. Soft teal glow is reserved for the dark gathering sequence. Paper and background stay low saturation.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows show real bounce squash, staggered document arrivals, a hard light/dark state change, per-letter assembly/dispersal and a stepped strip return to white. Ghost trajectories should be evaluated from past absolute times, so seeking produces the same result.

## Pace and density

This story-led film gives documents and comparison curves longer holds than abstract word cards. The ending is generous and calm, allowing the brand to be read after the documents clear.
