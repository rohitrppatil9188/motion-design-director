## Typography and hierarchy

Wide heavy sans contrasts with a large narrow italic serif. Outline echoes and repeated text stay subordinate to the main word. The closing pairing is simpler than the montage, maintaining a clear reading hierarchy.

## Composition and spatial structure

Single central heroes are used for the spatial and fluid studies, while full-bleed lattice and typography create the density peak. The blob's position deliberately occludes some letter strokes, making foreground/background interaction part of the design.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cream, black and cobalt are recurrent; the fluid blob combines blue, red, violet and yellow through smooth shading. Gradient highlights follow the blob's form, while most other fields remain solid.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show line expansion at 1.72s, cream radial fill near 2.8s, a blue expansion through the grid near 5.4s and a black radial opening at 9.33s. Fluid motion needs stable material coordinates, restrained deformation frequency and coherent light direction.

## Pace and density

The blob and particle word each have a sustained interval. The late optical montage is fast and explicitly contrasts with the final quiet lockup. The film does not require all passages to share the same tempo density.
