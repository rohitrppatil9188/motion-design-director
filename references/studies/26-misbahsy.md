## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy sans headlines and large figures contrast with small mono-like code and labels. The closing brand name is lighter, allowing the earlier headline to remain the peak. Code is a visual proof metaphor, not necessarily readable at viewing speed.

## Composition and spatial structure

The diagram is an explicit input-centre-output arrangement. The light code card uses a disciplined left-text/right-window split. Peripheral chips form a background field with a clear empty centre.

## Colour, gradients and material

Dark violet, white and saturated purple anchor the film. Red and green are selective state colours in the routing diagram. Soft glows sit behind primary objects rather than reducing text contrast.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows confirm a letter-counter zoom around 3.65s, card expansion near 5.8s, staggered vertical strip occlusion near 8.93s and whole-layout collapse near 12.82s. Lines and state changes are sequential enough to express cause and response.

## Pace and density

The code scene lasts roughly three seconds; the large numbers arrive late and hold briefly. The transition language is less frantic than the abstract reels because the central idea needs explanation.
