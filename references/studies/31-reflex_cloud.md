## Typography and hierarchy

Serif type is central, not a minor accent. Individual letters are treated as physical/graphic objects before they reunite in the final word. Mono-style labels support the technical concept, while large single letters give each treatment a clear silhouette.

## Composition and spatial structure

The eye's pupil, diagram endpoints and depth corridor share a central focal path. The spectral section occupies a full-width horizontal band. The final small wordmark benefits from the preceding large isolated glyphs.

## Colour, gradients and material

Mostly black, white and violet, with a concentrated full-spectrum chapter. The spectrum is organised by colour fields and wave frequency rather than an arbitrary rainbow wash. Photographic iris detail and subtle halftone texture provide material contrast.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows show a zoom into the pupil, white/black value flips, a looped path simplifying to a line, the diagram endpoint becoming a perspective image plane and multiple letter treatments. Camera depth and near-plane clipping are essential to the corridor; motion blur is directional around travel, not permanently applied.

## Pace and density

The eye opening and route diagram have clear holds; the corridor creates a longer spatial acceleration. Individual letters form a short late montage, then the final name becomes stable and small.
