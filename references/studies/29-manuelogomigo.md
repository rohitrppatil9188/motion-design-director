## Typography and hierarchy

Large high-contrast serif headlines create a more refined voice than the dominant heavy sans examples. Small sans/mono labels clarify interface details. The final name is a clean sans, contrasting with the headline serif without adding another display style.

## Composition and spatial structure

White negative space, fine rules and small orange details carry the layout. Dark globe and stacked-plane scenes have a centre/right hero balanced by a left serif headline. UI pills stay isolated and legible.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, warm white and near-black form a restrained palette. Orange illumination and particle brightness create depth on dark scenes. The interface chapters are mostly flat, avoiding gratuitous neon effects.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show progress-bar expansion into a strip wipe around 3.73s, particle-to-globe reconfiguration, vertical wipe near 7.45s and orange radial fill at 9.3s. The same point vocabulary bridges a functional UI gesture and an abstract spatial scene.

## Pace and density

The upload and globe chapters each get enough time to establish their main idea. The infinity flow is a short visual reward, then the website and logo slow the ending. Serif readability is maintained by relatively stable headline positions.
