## Typography and hierarchy

A high-contrast serif identity and italic type study contrast with broad black display words. Thin ruled rows and small mono-like labels give the opening and closing card a publication-like organisation.

## Composition and spatial structure

The card's two-column grid becomes a literal grid of modules in the form study. Perspective preserves the array's structure as it becomes a miniature world. A central particle/ribbon sequence removes the grid briefly before the card restores it.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cobalt, yellow, cream and black are used as flat module colours; the extruded scene adds shaded side faces and shadows. Particle/ribbon scenes use warm luminous gradients against black.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Five-frame windows clearly show the type-to-modules breakup near 4.53s, planar tilt and extrusion near 6.7s, radial colour cover near 10.22s and swirling geometry returning to the card near 12.98s. The strongest handoff preserves each module's cell identity through the dimensional transition.

## Pace and density

Type gets nearly three seconds and the spatial world about 3.5 seconds, substantially longer than a flash card. The closing card gets two seconds, making the résumé premise meaningful.
