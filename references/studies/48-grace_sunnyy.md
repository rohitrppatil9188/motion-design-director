## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy wide sans stays consistent across headline, transition cards and final identity. Outline echoes change treatment without introducing another family. Small technical labels and the timeline reinforce the keyframe metaphor.

## Composition and spatial structure

The keyframe strip at the bottom provides continuity and a visual time reference. The faceted object and particle word are centrally framed, while the panel dashboard is a dense but aligned rectangle.

## Colour, gradients and material

Red/orange, cream and near-black are strongly controlled, with muted particle accents. Facets have discrete red value changes rather than smooth fluid gradients. Bright cream fields provide occasional value resets.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows show a graph-to-type transition, facet scattering/reassembly near 5.2s, diamond-to-particles at 5.95s, panels-to-circular reveal at 9.4s and a final diamond carrier around 11.85s. The transition demo should be understood as a catalogue, not an instruction to use every effect in one new film.

## Pace and density

The film has a clear first proposition, material middle, faster transition sampler and an unusually generous final hold. Dense motion settles before the identity becomes readable.
