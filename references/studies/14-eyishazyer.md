## Typography and hierarchy

Wide black/cream uppercase headlines anchor the film. Numbers have comparable weight, making the countdown and dashboard coherent. Small section labels consistently sit near the upper-left rather than changing position on every card.

## Composition and spatial structure

The rhythm matrix uses a stable grid and a moving bright region; dimensional loops occupy a central safe area. The data layout balances left figures, centre bars and right ring. The end mark is smaller and quieter than the montage.

## Colour, gradients and material

The palette is broad, but each chapter has a dominant field. Pink/cyan curves and violet illumination are true gradient/glow material effects. Orange topography and cream data panels provide flatter intervals that keep the film from becoming uniformly luminous.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

The windows expose strip wipes around 3.6s and 9.18s, a portal handoff near 5.57s and a diagonal wipe near 7.33s. The topographic study can use phase-shifted line displacement; the rhythm matrix can modulate cell radius and roundness by distance to a moving annulus.

## Pace and density

The countdown invests in anticipation, most major studies get roughly two seconds, and the final principles run is much faster. The film separates motion density from useful reading time.
