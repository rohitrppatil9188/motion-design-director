## Typography and hierarchy

Wide bold sans and outline echoes create a consistent headline system. Colour is assigned to selected letters and the final small mark, while the main closing name stays neutral. The topographic scene keeps the word above the line field.

## Composition and spatial structure

A central hero is always distinguishable from its supporting lattice or orbit. The three-card layout is clean and aligned, providing a quiet bridge between two particle/line passages. Perimeter technical furniture remains secondary.

## Colour, gradients and material

Lime, coral/orange, cyan, violet, pink, cream and black form a broad identity. Chapters choose one dominant field, while line/particle colours distribute the secondary hues. The topographic scene uses coloured illumination and gradient depth rather than flat graphic dots.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows show layered circle fills, a black iris near 5.48s, particle depth expansion, a bright UI reveal near 9.4s and dark circular cover near 11.1s. Stable point indices and a common projection make sphere/torus/cube changes legible.

## Pace and density

The middle has several distinct two-second studies, while the closing line field accelerates before a clean band wipe and stable identity. The UI panels are a lower-density pause, not a separate product walkthrough.
