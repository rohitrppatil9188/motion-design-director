## Typography and hierarchy

Wide heavy statement type alternates with narrower outline/striped lettering in the procedural field. The final mixed-case title is softer than the opening caps. The montage uses type as a texture/silhouette rather than a paragraph.

## Composition and spatial structure

Centred shape studies keep the surrounding procedural fields readable. Fine corner furniture and a baseline continue across the reel. The playful ball is grounded by a shadow, unlike the floating mathematical point volumes.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cobalt, lime, cream and black are mostly flat chapter fields. The ball adds restrained highlight shading; optical pattern sections rely on contrast and line density, not gradients.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows show a stepped wipe at 3.43s, a palette inversion around 5.63s, a grid-to-flow reorientation near 7.65s and blue iris expansion near 9.35s. The topology studies need stable point IDs; the line field needs a smooth direction function with phase continuity.

## Pace and density

The middle progresses from flat shape to field to volume, then offers a brief playful break before a rapid optical climax. A calmer final name counterbalances the dense pattern section.
