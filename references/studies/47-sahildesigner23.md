## Typography and hierarchy

The film explicitly demonstrates weight and dimensionality within one broad sans family. Extruded sides are orange while front faces stay cream. The final mixed-case name is less aggressive than the all-caps opening.

## Composition and spatial structure

A strong horizontal word band becomes a spatial object. The phone is isolated with a cast shadow; the tile scene has a stable plane and camera angle. The editor framing is a presentation device, not assumed proof of real software use.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cream and black dominate, with silver/chrome reflections. The palette's restraint makes material changes clear. Fluid highlights and reflected warm colour are genuine shading cues, unlike the flat outline-text wallpaper.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows show a counter passage near 5.47s, fluid-to-device silhouette near 7.37s and device-screen-to-tile scale match near 9.17s. Extruded type requires coherent front/back geometry and side faces; offset flat shadows are only an approximation.

## Pace and density

This source spends longer on material and spatial scenes, with relatively little flash-card montage. The final name holds nearly two seconds after a short transition reset.
