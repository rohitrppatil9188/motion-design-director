## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy wide sans carries the primary titles, while a large narrow italic serif appears within the spatial plane and a small italic subtitle appears at the end. Text filled with chrome imagery is a clipping-mask material effect, distinct from extruded 3D type.

## Composition and spatial structure

The modular grid is compact with large cream margins. The spatial plane maintains a readable central composition as its camera changes; the chrome sphere is isolated so its reflections remain legible. The flourish gives the ending a directional baseline.

## Colour, gradients and material

Warm cream, black, coral and cobalt form the graphic palette. The material scene uses genuine reflected-light gradients and a narrow warm rim. A coloured background gradient alone cannot reproduce a metallic object.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows expose iris expansion at 2.48s, staggered module flips, panel expansion near 4.82s, depth-plane parallax and camera passage near 7.7s, then oblique occlusion near 10.3s. Deterministic projection, clipping and depth sorting matter more than adding arbitrary spring motion.

## Pace and density

The material scene is allowed roughly 2.5 seconds of visual attention. Several very short cards form a bridge to an ending with a long readable hold. The flourish continues gently without destabilising the final text.
