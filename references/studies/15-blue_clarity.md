## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed capitals make large words fit in a narrow height/width system, while outline and particle treatments retain the same recognisable proportions. The final lockup returns to solid sharp text; small curve labels remain peripheral.

## Composition and spatial structure

Axes, corner marks and a small top-right curve preview create continuity. The nine-cell composition is an explicit climax of parallel activity, bracketed by single-object studies and a quiet end card.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cobalt and yellow accent a pale grey/navy foundation. Most graphic fields are flat. Particle brightness and thin coloured rings create dimensional variation without a broad background gradient.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

The windows confirm radial fill at 1.98s, a bar/word-strip exit near 3.93s, a dramatic radial particle expansion at 6.08s, stepped diagonal occlusion at 8.45s and panel enlargement at 13.02s. Geometric shapes retain consistent centres and vertex correspondence through the morph.

## Pace and density

The middle particle study is longer than a title impact, while the wall of nine examples gives a dense but stable composition. The last two seconds restore a single clear identity.
