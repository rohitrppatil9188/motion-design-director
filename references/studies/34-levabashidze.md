## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy grotesk is paired with restrained italic serif accents. The main statement is left aligned, with consistent line lengths and a clear curve-diagram counterpart. The large progress percentage is a graphic device, not a log readout.

## Composition and spatial structure

Many scenes use one central shape and generous margins. The isometric plane's cell structure is visible before height changes begin. The final lockup returns to a conventional editorial baseline after dense pattern and text fields.

## Colour, gradients and material

Muted orange/red, warm cream, near-black and cobalt. Mostly flat fills, with shaded cube side faces and low-opacity shape echoes. The restrained palette lets procedural pattern carry the complexity.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows reveal a stepped cream wipe at 3.55s, cube-to-plane scale continuity at 5.63s, a perspective plane turning front-on near 7.28s and letter-counter iris near 11.15s. Tile height can be a travelling radial wave with delayed phase per cell.

## Pace and density

The film alternates approximate two-second studies, with a slower first explanation and final name. A short progress metaphor recaps rather than adding a new unrelated effect.
