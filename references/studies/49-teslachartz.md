## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed sans anchors the opening and ending. The montage briefly demonstrates outline and dimensional type. The grid and curve scene use fine mono-like annotations, kept outside the main silhouettes.

## Composition and spatial structure

Regular shape rows create a stable comparison before the spatial torus. The timing graphic has a clean left-curve/right-lanes hierarchy. The final type band restores simple alignment after the richer montage.

## Colour, gradients and material

Red/orange, cobalt, lime, cream and black. The torus has discrete faceted shading, while the fluid contours are flat lime silhouettes with thin nested lines. This contrast is more useful than treating both as a generic gradient effect.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show a strip wipe near 3.42s, square/blue enlargement near 5.48s, torus fragmentation near 7.5s and lime iris near 9.4s. A torus shader/wireframe reveal should share one underlying mesh so the surface does not jump between representations.

## Pace and density

The main grid, torus and fluid chapters each get about two seconds. A short type montage accelerates before a long final name hold.
