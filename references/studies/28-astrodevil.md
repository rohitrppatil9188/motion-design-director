## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy slightly rounded grotesk headlines and figures provide the voice. Outline display words create occasional contrast without changing the family. Small mono-like labels and chips support the numeric layouts.

## Composition and spatial structure

Most scenes use left-aligned type with a right-side graphic. The terminal and closing logo are smaller central objects. Persistent fine frame furniture and a warm low-contrast texture unify the sequence.

## Colour, gradients and material

Cream text on near-black, with burnt orange/red gradients and glows. Bright orange full fields are used for specific proof cards. The warm glow has a low-value edge, preserving foreground readability.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows expose diagonal wipes near 3.7s, 7.45s and 11.2s, plus short bright resets around 1.88s and 5.63s. Count-up figures settle before the scene leaves, while supporting chips enter with a delay.

## Pace and density

The first proposition and large-count scenes have useful reading time. The capability words are faster, then the closing name slows down. The sequence is designed around hierarchy, not continuous maximum velocity.
