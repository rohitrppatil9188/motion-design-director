## Typography and hierarchy

Condensed heavy type saves vertical space; an italic serif introduces a more conversational voice. Repeated solid/outline words form a poster texture. The final title is large relative to the narrow frame but preserves side margins.

## Composition and spatial structure

Portrait framing uses tall matrices and stacked poster bands rather than shrinking a landscape design. Corner marks remain secondary. Single central forms alternate with full-height fields; the card is a deliberate brief change in scale.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cobalt, cream and black carry the film. Fluid badges introduce iridescent gradients, coloured edge reflections and glossy highlights; these richer materials are concentrated in one passage.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows reveal a radial fill near 1.45s, a text-wall exit near 4s, blue iris reduction near 5.95s, bright material transition near 10s and orange/white card reveal near 11.78s. Preserve a common central axis through these scale changes.

## Pace and density

The first four seconds are typographic, the next six are progressively spatial, and the final five combine one material peak, one data card and a clear identity. The clip feels varied without changing every layer simultaneously.
