## Typography and hierarchy

A wide black sans provides impact, while an italic serif makes the explanatory curve scene and final role line more editorial. Outline repeats are background structure. Orbital lettering is individually positioned, scaled and depth-sorted rather than merely rotated as one flat line.

## Composition and spatial structure

The curve-and-lanes diagram has an explicit left/right explanation hierarchy. Dense grid studies and central dimensional objects are bracketed by large clean title fields. Corner marks continue across chapters.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cream and black dominate; cobalt/blue is a distinct spatial accent. A soft blue core illuminates particles, but the matrix and words remain graphic and mostly flat. Lime is used sparingly in the closing badge.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show band expansion at 1.52s, matched circle-to-square lattice transformations, grid collapse near 6.7s and a spatial helix enlargement into a cream diagram near 9.5s. Use varying glyph z values for the type orbit; hide or dim the rear half to preserve depth.

## Pace and density

The first montage is rapid, the lattice gets about two seconds, and the curve demonstration gets a longer readable interval. A final short burst precedes the calmer identity.
