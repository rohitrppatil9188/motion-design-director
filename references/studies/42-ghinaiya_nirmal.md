## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy sans carries headline cards, while a large italic serif defines the fluid passage and final role. Outline repetitions and small technical labels support rather than replace the main hierarchy.

## Composition and spatial structure

Selection bounds make the morphs look constructed; dot fields have a stable row/column reference. Circle clusters intentionally overlap the serif word. The later curve is isolated, giving a low-density pause before the montage.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cobalt, pink and lime accent cream/black. The cell field uses colour as a moving spatial signal. The fluid passage is primarily flat coloured silhouettes, not a reflective 3D material.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows expose triangle-to-star correspondence, moving colour annuli through cells, a cream expansion at 9.35s and a drip mask descending around 11.15s. A drip wipe can use a shared leading edge plus smoothly varying per-column droplet length; random vertical rectangles would read differently.

## Pace and density

The cell and point studies have time to evolve; the final montage is brief and varied. The last identity holds after all major transformations settle.
