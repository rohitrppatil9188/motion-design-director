## Typography and hierarchy

Wide heavy sans and narrow italic serif create strong scale/texture contrast. The circular typography is densely repeated, but the finale returns to plain baseline type. Outline bands behind the glossy object remain lower in hierarchy.

## Composition and spatial structure

The glossy object is centrally isolated on a subtle perspective floor; background text supplies width. The topographic and bar sections switch to clear planar information layouts. Radial rings provide the final acceleration.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cream, cobalt and black dominate. The object uses white specular patches and red/blue/yellow reflections. Particle colour and glows are warmer; chart fields are flatter and cleaner.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Five-frame windows confirm counter passage near 3.62s, a shape-matched light cut at 5.63s, radial particle expansion near 7.53s and line-to-chart enlargement near 10.57s. The final rings combine scale and angular phase, with temporary blur during speed rather than throughout readable type.

## Pace and density

The glossy object has about two seconds; the grid and particle word also get coherent passages. The radial climax is shorter than the preceding studies and resolves into a calm identity.
