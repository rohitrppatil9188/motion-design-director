## Typography and hierarchy

An unusually disciplined combination of condensed sans, broad bold sans and high-contrast serif is managed through scale and role. The final serif wordmark is dominant and spacious, giving the film an editorial rather than generic tech identity.

## Composition and spatial structure

The same grid underlies multiplication, bars, tiles, dots and branching choices. Cream negative space is deliberate. Figures often sit in a corner, creating asymmetric balance rather than default central alignment.

## Colour, gradients and material

Cobalt and warm cream do nearly all the work, with near-black explanatory text. Facet shading and halftone value changes add depth without introducing new hues. The lack of gradient decoration makes structural motion more visible.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows confirm square-array growth, a large typographic reveal, bar-to-diagram transition, isometric-to-front-on grid rotation, density increase and circle-to-blue wipe. Reuse cell coordinates across square, dot, height and halftone representations.

## Pace and density

Each idea has a distinct interval, with a readable slower final serif composition. The running pictogram is a brief kinetic bridge, not the film's narrative subject.
