## Typography and hierarchy

Bold sans-serif handles principle cards and numerical hierarchy; outline repetitions extend it into texture. A large italic serif becomes the emotional contrast in the ribbon scene. The closing lowercase wordmark changes scale rather than competing with the climax.

## Composition and spatial structure

Corner marks and bottom rules persist across styles. The visual hierarchy alternates centred hero forms with a left-number/right-chart editorial split. Ribbons stay behind the type, leaving a clean central reading zone.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, warm white and cobalt organise flat graphic sections. Dark particle scenes use low-value blue/purple; the ribbon passage introduces genuine semi-transparent colour gradients and layered shading.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Transition windows show a seed-driven radial fill at 1.8s, offset bar curtains at 3.9s and 9.2s, and horizontal stripe occlusion at 11.1s. Particle text is an attractor construction; a stable point set should morph to sampled glyph positions rather than flickering through fresh random samples each frame.

## Pace and density

The first principle words are brisk, the particle word holds long enough to read, and the richer ribbon phrase is sustained for about two seconds. The last lockup gets a quieter final section.
