## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed capitals are counterbalanced by broad red circular letterforms. Outline repeats remain thin enough to sit behind the main word. The montage mixes scripts briefly, but the final identity returns to the primary condensed family.

## Composition and spatial structure

A stable centre carries iris transitions and point volumes. The typography uses intentional cropping and full-height letters for impact, with fine technical furniture near the perimeter. The closing lockup has smaller scale and more breathing room.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange/red, cream, cobalt, black and acid lime mark distinct chapters. Most fields are flat; subtle glows and colour fringes appear in particle/impact passages. Strong value contrasts do more work than continuous gradients.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Five-frame windows show a stepped horizontal wipe near 1.83s, a letter-counter-to-grid zoom at 3.87s and radial black opening at 5.8s. The transition is strongest when the circular counter's centre and radius align with the incoming portal.

## Pace and density

The shape lattice and particle volume each get roughly two seconds. The typography montage is faster, then the particle-to-solid title rebuild lets the film slow down again. The final solid lockup is held for substantially longer than a montage word.
