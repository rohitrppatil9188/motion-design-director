## Typography and hierarchy

Bold friendly grotesk and a simple two-line hierarchy make this more accessible than the heavy technical showreels. Smaller labels and chart categories remain aligned. Letter blur is a short arrival effect, not a permanent style.

## Composition and spatial structure

The film alternates text-left/map-right, count-left/chart-right and a full-frame card plane. White space and a pale dotted grid preserve clarity. The selected card separates through depth, scale and sharpness.

## Colour, gradients and material

Teal, pale mint, black and white create a coherent small palette. Shadows and a faint teal halo provide subtle depth. Gradients are secondary; large flat fields and clean contrast carry the design.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows show word-by-word settling, an organic teal/white map reveal around 2.57s, stepped occlusion near 5.1s, camera tilt over cards and contraction of a full-frame teal shape into the logo icon. The card field requires stable positions and selective focus, not per-card random movement.

## Pace and density

Longer explanatory scenes suit the product story; the end has a long readable hold. This is a useful applied-motion contrast case, with less visual experimentation than the strongest abstract references.
