## Typography and hierarchy

Large relatively light grotesk category words contrast with the heavier bold display type of most references. Image-filled type is a mask treatment. The final studio name is thin and spacious, consistent with the portfolio's more corporate tone.

## Composition and spatial structure

Full-frame project samples are separated by camera moves through angled mockup walls. Black negative space carries the claim card and closing identity. The proof layout uses clear columns rather than overlaid decorative effects.

## Colour, gradients and material

Cobalt is the studio accent; green and multicolour project imagery introduce temporary palettes. White device scenes provide a high-value break. A dark blue glow is restricted to the closing background.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows confirm rectangle expansion, type-mask zoom-through near 2.22s, several direct project cuts, a phone-strip arrival near 8.52s and whole-layout reduction near 13s. Perspective and overscan are essential for mockup walls; individual projects should remain attached to their planes.

## Pace and density

Rapid project glimpses make the portfolio feel broad, while the claims and final logo get longer holds. It is appropriate when showcasing actual supplied work, less suitable for a purely abstract showreel brief.
