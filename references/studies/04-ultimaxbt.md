**Visual thesis.** A single orange dot links an accessibility-toggle joke, typography, spring mechanics, shape transformations, a dimensional type ring and a halftone field. The return to the opening composition makes this an intentional loop.

## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy rounded grotesk in mixed case makes the film friendlier than the condensed editorial examples. The ring uses bold uppercase text, while claims use a strong left-aligned hierarchy. The on-screen zero-keyframe claim is content, not verified production evidence.

## Composition and spatial structure

The dot stays central in geometry studies, then becomes punctuation in left-aligned text. The cream field, graph baseline and wide margins prevent the small system from looking empty.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange is the sole vivid accent, balanced against cream and near-black. Soft edge glow and smears are transient; the design does not depend on a multicolour background gradient.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows around 5.1s show a graph-to-line enlargement preserving the terminal dot; 5.65s is a palette cut around the same silhouette; 9.42s keeps the seed through a type-to-halftone cut. The halftone ripple modulates radius by distance. Perspective ring depth can be recreated with z-scaled glyphs, opacity and correct far-to-near drawing.

## Pace and density

Demonstrations last roughly 1.5–2 seconds, with a longer readable credentials section before the fast return. Loop design is visible in the matching opening and near-final toggle, though audio-loop seamlessness was not auditioned.
