## Typography and hierarchy

Wide heavy uppercase type handles identity, while an exceptionally tall number and condensed repeated text briefly demonstrate other proportions. Fine mono-style annotations tie the graphic demonstrations to a technical workspace.

## Composition and spatial structure

The point/line/plane section establishes a centre and scale. Orbit ellipses supply depth cues without competing with the sphere. The style cards change composition sharply but preserve corner marks, accent and an orderly frame.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange/red and cream dominate, with cobalt restricted to selected style cards and blocks. The point-sphere section is mostly neutral on black. Soft warm glow appears at the ending, subordinate to readable type.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Transition windows show a line-to-plane expansion at 1.87s, visible planar rotation, a name-to-particle seed handoff around 5.33s and a bar-to-full-card wipe at 9.2s. The radial shape study can use sampled polar radii with identical vertex counts and rotational alignment.

## Pace and density

The foundational geometry gets nearly four seconds; the middle sphere and bar graph have distinct holds; the style montage is intentionally faster. The closing lockup gets about two seconds.

## Transferable construction

Teach spatial complexity as a progression of dimensions. A line becoming a plane is more intelligible than cutting from unrelated flat type directly to an arbitrary 3D object.
