**Visual thesis.** A vertical maximalist collage uses visual overload to embody an overthinking theme, then earns relief with a sparse circular closing emblem. It is a deliberate stylistic contrast to the Swiss/technical reels.

## Typography and hierarchy

Outlined/shadowed capitals remain readable against busy photographic collage, while small italic serif text supplies a contrasting intimate voice. Repeated headline fragments become texture rather than new information. The ending reduces both type count and graphic density.

## Composition and spatial structure

Strong vertical symmetry and a persistent central beam stop the many objects from losing their axis. The closing circular crop compresses earlier complexity into one recognisable silhouette. Native portrait framing is essential; a 16:9 adaptation needs recomposition, not letterboxing.

## Colour, gradients and material

Gold, yellow, electric blue, coral and rainbow beams create a psychedelic palette. Glows, gradients, noisy texture and poster-like colour separation are part of the material system. Black margins provide relief.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

The windows reveal short bright exposure-like flashes, vertical zooms, horizontal glitch strips and a graphic contact-sheet montage. Some scene changes are straight cuts hidden by brightness or colour disruption. This is collage motion and layered compositing, not continuous shape morphing throughout.

## Pace and density

A restrained first second makes the next ten seconds feel much louder. A near-black reset around 12.2s is a structural breath before the closing emblem. Many individual collage panels are intentionally read as impression rather than text.
