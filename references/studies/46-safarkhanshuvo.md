## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed sans is the central voice, with a lighter wide word on the gradient card. The final title's proportion matches the earlier particle text. Small technical labels stay at the perimeter.

## Composition and spatial structure

The grid retains visible row logic through deformation. Data is balanced across the blue field; nested rectangles have a coherent vanishing point. The final mark is separate from the punctuation dot and uses a distinct silhouette.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, blue, lime, cream and black recur. A short smooth card uses a genuine soft warm/cool gradient; other cards rely on flat fields and high-contrast pattern. The contrast between those material treatments is intentional.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show radial orange expansion at 1.65s, slice-to-matrix wipe near 3.63s, darkening grid transition near 5.45s and a blue iris through the particle word at 7.35s. The tunnel needs stable depth spacing and clipping at the camera plane.

## Pace and density

Clear two-second middle studies lead into a shorter optical montage. The wireframe word sequence creates a sustained final acceleration, then a lighter ending restores readability.
