## Typography and hierarchy

The main type is wide and heavy. The tower uses uppercase bands with sufficient tracking for each glyph's tangent placement. An italic serif accent is restricted to the final small line.

## Composition and spatial structure

The cylinder is centred with enough top/bottom space to read its stacked bands; camera roll and perspective reveal the rear surface. The panel wall is a structured rectangle, not an arbitrary collage.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, cream, black, cobalt, lime and pink recur. The tower's colours are assigned by band, making depth and grouping easier to follow. Most fields are flat; particle intensity adds local luminance variation.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows show counter zoom at 3.58s, square-to-grid scale passage at 5.67s, a diagonal cell opening near 7.13s and panel-to-particle release at 10.85s. Project each tower glyph from a cylinder, sort by z and attenuate the rear half; rotating a flat text block cannot reproduce it.

## Pace and density

The tower occupies about two seconds and the particle ending another two, while early words are shorter. The final name is readable after the noisy assembly has finished.
