## Typography and hierarchy

A heavy editorial serif is the primary voice, paired with small mono-like technical labels. The flip-tile passage is the main sans/mono display contrast. Blue emphasis is selective, not applied to every line.

## Composition and spatial structure

A left-headline/right-analysis layout recurs. Faint grids and technical corners create a consistent frame. The histogram, radar and UI each have a protected empty margin around them, preserving hierarchy.

## Colour, gradients and material

Near-black blue, cream and cool blue dominate; green/magenta are small interface states. Flare and radar illumination are local gradients, while the main reading surfaces remain low-noise.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show a line-to-title handoff, particle-to-distribution assembly, flare-to-icon reduction and a letter-tile reveal. The radar wedge is a rotating angular mask with dim persistent rings; its chart should draw independently instead of being treated as another spinning object.

## Pace and density

Several longer explanatory passages make this a narrative/product reference. The flip tiles are a brief late variation, then the brand returns with a stable hold.
