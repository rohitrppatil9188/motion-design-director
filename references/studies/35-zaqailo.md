## Typography and hierarchy

A wide heavy sans anchors identity, outline echoes and short cards. A serif type card is a deliberate contrast. Red punctuation and one red initial create a recurring point of emphasis.

## Composition and spatial structure

The centre holds through counter zoom, particles, facets and fluid objects. Thin orbital lines and grid furniture describe scale. The material studies leave sufficient negative space for silhouettes to remain visible.

## Colour, gradients and material

Cream/black and orange/cobalt/yellow connect flat tiles and spatial objects. The low-poly surface uses discrete facet colours; the smooth sphere uses continuous warm-to-blue shading and a coherent highlight. These are different material models.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows confirm letter-counter passage near 3.65s, tile disassembly near 5.48s, facet explosion/reassembly around 8s and smooth-object scale cover at 11.15s. Preserve vertex/particle identities during assembly so the transition reads as transformation rather than fresh random geometry.

## Pace and density

The representation changes are sustained long enough to compare, with the quickest typography near the end. The final identity gets a calmer roughly two-second interval.
