## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy sans headlines dominate, with outline echoes used as rhythm and texture. The blob word changes foreground treatment according to whether it overlaps light background or saturated colour. Small mono-style metadata stays in the framing layer.

## Composition and spatial structure

The radial mark gives the dense opening a centre. Grids are regular enough that deformation is visible; blob and particle forms return to a single hero silhouette. The UI arrangement is a controlled composition, not a scrolling screen recording.

## Colour, gradients and material

Coral, electric blue, violet and acid lime are prominent. The fluid blob contains genuine soft colour gradients and shifting highlights; the word-card fields use mostly solid fills. The rainbow matrix distributes colour systematically across rows/radii.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Events show a radial fill near 1.97s, oblique strip occlusion near 3.95s, cream expansion at 5.98s and reverse iris around 7.95s. Consistent seeded grid cells can interpolate radius, roundness and hue; a shader or layered radial gradients can provide the blob's interior without adding unrelated background glow.

## Pace and density

The fluid and particle chapters have enough time for a full shape change, while the last two seconds before the logo use short typographic impacts. The lockup is clear and quieter than the climax.
