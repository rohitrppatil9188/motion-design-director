## Typography and hierarchy

The main sequence combines very heavy uppercase sans headlines, widely tracked uppercase circular bands and a contrasting serif closing wordmark. These are separate functional voices. Text on an arc keeps its baseline tangent to the circle rather than rotating an entire rectangular line.

## Composition and spatial structure

The visual centre is unusually stable even when objects rotate. Full-bleed colour fields frame solitary mid-scale forms; denser dot and type fields appear between quiet single-object shots. Corner marks provide continuity without becoming the subject.

## Colour, gradients and material

Coral, electric blue, yellow, warm cream and black form clearly separated chapters. The tube is genuinely shaded and banded; most backgrounds remain flat. The small coral seed maintains identity across the palette changes.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native-frame windows reveal circular matte expansion at about 1.9s and 13.17s, fast text distortion near 2.83s, a type-to-dot reduction near 3.75s and an abrupt cream-to-cobalt change while the point-grid structure persists at 7.5s. Shape continuity matters more than a generic dissolve.

## Pace and density

The middle section demonstrates a new spatial family roughly every two seconds, then tightens into a ring of text before a calmer final two seconds. Pauses on recognisable silhouettes make the more complex point transformations understandable.
