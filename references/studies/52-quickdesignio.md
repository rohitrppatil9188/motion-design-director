## Typography and hierarchy

A broad clean sans carries statements and feature words; italic serif gives the lower ad-wall line and ending a more editorial tone. Type size is controlled so the imagery and headline have distinct roles.

## Composition and spatial structure

The ad wall is a single angled camera object with aligned rows, while the before/after view uses a simple side-by-side reveal. The final mark derives from the cell system rather than appearing unrelated at the end.

## Colour, gradients and material

Lime and black dominate, with cream and coral as contrasting card fields. Product imagery adds temporary colours, but the frame and type system remain lime/ink. Soft green illumination is local and restrained.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show the input-to-wall reset, phased card population, a lateral before/after reveal, a short word-card montage and a full-frame cell field resolving into the logo. Use stable cell correspondence and a designed target layout for the final assembly.

## Pace and density

The wall and before/after scenes get about two seconds each; the feature words are faster, and the growth card slows the pace before the modular identity reveal. This is an applied brand-motion example, not a pure abstract showreel.
