## Typography and hierarchy

A rounded bold sans carries the primary sentence and figures; an italic serif selectively emphasises one word or line. The thin monogram contrasts with the text's mass. Outline numbers support the case-card sequence.

## Composition and spatial structure

Landscape photographs and portrait panels are carefully cropped rather than uniformly scaled. Titles retain a left-side reading zone. Fine corner/timecode furniture creates continuity across portfolio assets.

## Colour, gradients and material

Warm sunset photography gives way to teal/navy and pale grey. Muted green/gold strips bridge imagery and brand colour. A soft blue/teal closing glow supports the white monogram without reducing contrast.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows reveal monogram zoom-through at 1.47s, sliding image panels, vertical strip occlusion around 4.1s, website-panel assembly near 7.1s and camera tilt near 8.7s. The best transitions use existing panel edges as masks.

## Pace and density

The opening sentence gets time to build, the case studies are brisk, and the final studio statement is slower. This is a portfolio/brand application example rather than a pure abstract geometry reel.
