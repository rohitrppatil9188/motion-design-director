## Typography and hierarchy

Very tall, narrow, black uppercase display type creates impact without requiring a full-width line. A blue handwritten/italic annotation style explains the action in a smaller register. Individual character timing is visible; the row can settle while a trailing character still moves.

## Composition and spatial structure

A baseline and timeline/keyframe strip keep the film in a motion-editor metaphor. Titles occupy a large left/central block; tiny notes sit near the affected object rather than becoming generic captions. A fixed baseline makes squash and displacement legible.

## Colour, gradients and material

White and charcoal alternate with blue as the recurring primary accent and red as a selective comparison/correction cue. Subtle grey vignetting appears on light fields, but the main colour story is high-contrast flat type.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

The sampled transition frames show real staggered arrivals and recoveries, a horizontal white wipe near 5.53s, a vertical wipe near 8.4s and concentric expansion near 13.13s. Ghost poses communicate trajectories; they should fade by age and stay behind the current object. The displayed annotations are source content, not independently verified timing settings.

## Pace and density

A quiet physical opening leads into a rapid sequence of four action words, followed by a longer diagram explanation and a flowing spatial passage. This alternation prevents continuous high-energy text from exhausting the viewer.
