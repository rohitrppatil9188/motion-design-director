## Typography and hierarchy

Bold wide sans and selected yellow words create a clear headline hierarchy. Circular type is the most elaborate typographic study, while the final name returns to a straightforward baseline. Faint outline wallpaper is kept behind the primary reading layer.

## Composition and spatial structure

Distinct chapters alternate single centred forms with structured panels. The multi-panel recap works because its cells are recognisable earlier scenes. Sparse geometric accents at the end recall the colour system without recreating the entire montage.

## Colour, gradients and material

Orange, blue, yellow, cream and black recur. The blue blob uses soft internal shading against flat yellow; topographic lines introduce a controlled multicolour sweep. Background gradients remain subordinate to the hero.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows show line expansion, circular blue fill, diagonal wipe, concentric type rotation/zoom and a panel-wall depth exit. Type rings need per-glyph placement and bounded angular speed; topographic curves need continuous phase across lines.

## Pace and density

The rings receive an unusually generous central interval. The final panel wall compresses the full visual inventory into a brief climax, followed by a stable logo. Data cards are held as a clear three-part composition.
