## Typography and hierarchy

Very heavy rounded lowercase type is the primary voice. It feels friendlier and more compact than condensed all-caps. Numerical cards use similar mass. Small sans role text stays clearly secondary.

## Composition and spatial structure

The ball is the focal anchor across flat, isometric and radial scenes. White negative space makes the later circular graphics feel clean. The small closing lockup is a deliberate scale reduction after the tunnel.

## Colour, gradients and material

Cobalt, white, near-black, lime and orange. Extruded tiles use shaded side faces, and the orange spheres use glossy highlights. The rest of the film is predominantly flat graphic colour.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows confirm shape-grid growth, a planar-to-isometric pivot, contact-centred height waves, sphere merging/splitting and a particle-ring-to-data-ring match. Match radius, centre and angular phase at the ring handoff to avoid a visible jump.

## Pace and density

The isometric passage lasts around two seconds; the circular tunnel gets a longer late interval. The final name has a generous hold of nearly three seconds, reinforcing confidence rather than forcing another effect.
