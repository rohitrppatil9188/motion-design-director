## Typography and hierarchy

Heavy uppercase sans carries category names, narrow italic serif provides editorial contrast, and mono-like text handles prompt/number fields. The image-filled letters reuse the same material world as the hero imagery.

## Composition and spatial structure

Type and media share a grid: title, chapter number, rule and supporting label repeat. The angled media wall is a density climax; later split layouts restore hierarchy. The white ending is a strong value and density reset.

## Colour, gradients and material

Violet and near-black dominate, with silver highlights and pink reflections. These highlights are material gradients; neutral white text stays clear. The closing white field makes the violet brand symbol feel deliberate.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows show strip expansion around 1s, direct imagery cuts, lateral text sweeps, a zoom from dimensional lettering into a media wall around 5.8s and a layered radial-mask closing transition. Material-filled type should be clipped consistently and retain a stable silhouette.

## Pace and density

The film moves through short categories, a longer imagery/claim middle and a compact demo, then holds the logo. Not every background image needs to be read in full; the hierarchy distinguishes showcase texture from the primary sentence.
