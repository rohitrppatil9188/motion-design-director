## Typography and hierarchy

Tall condensed sans is consistently used for the main name and principle words. An orange letter and a small role line create hierarchy without a new family. Faint outline text is only a background anchor in the silhouette scene.

## Composition and spatial structure

The centred orbital system carries most chapters. Lime provides a strong silhouette field; the wireframe object has enough negative space for the rear/front geometry to read. Thin technical furniture remains peripheral.

## Colour, gradients and material

Cream/orange, violet and lime on near-black. The galaxy's luminous centre and dimmer arms create real value falloff. The broad graphic fields are largely flat, with a few vignette/gradient transitions.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Native windows show slice removal near 3.5s, expanding/rotating cell fields, lime reveal around 5.55s, radial transition near 7.5s and torus enlargement into the closing reset. Wireframe visibility needs depth sorting or hidden-surface attenuation so its structure remains understandable.

## Pace and density

Most major studies receive about two seconds, the principle cards are briefer and the final mark holds. Orbital speed and particle density change independently, giving the middle a different feel from the word cards.
