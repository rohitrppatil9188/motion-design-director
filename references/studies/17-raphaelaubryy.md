## Typography and hierarchy

A conventional heavy sans remains consistent from title through repeated wallpaper and cube faces. The film relies on compositional changes rather than many typefaces. A small italic label occasionally introduces a study without competing with the headline.

## Composition and spatial structure

The hero stays central with broad margins; the grid and wallpaper briefly fill the frame. Cube-face typography is perspective-projected with the surface, rather than pasted over the screen. Thin construction circles unify the opening morphs.

## Colour, gradients and material

Desaturated coral, cream and near-black make a restrained, warm palette. Grey arises through dim cells and particles. The merging white shapes are primarily silhouette effects; no complex rainbow gradient is required.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Reviewed windows expose a zoom-through type transition near 1.85s, a grid reveal near 4s, particle release near 6s and a label-wall-to-cube scale reduction near 12s. The fluid section can be built from a thresholded scalar field whose sources follow independent but smooth orbits.

## Pace and density

Most studies receive about two seconds and the cube turns at a measured speed. This example shows that visual confidence can come from clear transformations and held silhouettes rather than rapid flashing.
