## Typography and hierarchy

A heavy wide sans handles headlines and the final name. Outline repetitions form background texture. Most explanatory detail is in the curve/dot diagram rather than in additional text paragraphs.

## Composition and spatial structure

The regular matrix and nested polygons each use an obvious centre. Persistent corner marks and a fine lower rule maintain the technical frame. Grain is visible even on otherwise simple flat fields.

## Colour, gradients and material

Coral/orange, cobalt, lime and magenta provide strong chapter contrast. The fluid material uses broad pink highlight patches and blue edging; this should be distinguished from a smooth specular shader. Background grain gives a common surface.

## Motion mechanics and transitions

Windows show line-to-colour expansion, shape matrix rotation, a black iris near 6s, semicircular occlusion near 8s and a bright reset at 10.5s. Tunnel polygons need a coherent vanishing point and clipped near-plane behaviour; independently scaling random polygons would weaken the spatial read.

## Pace and density

Short early typography accelerates into two-second demonstrations. The diagram and fluid shape have clearer holds, and the last identity is calmer. Some titles are only captured during transition windows, so not every sample is a readable poster frame.

## Transferable construction

Texture can unify otherwise diverse scenes, but use a consistent restrained grain treatment. Let each effect have a clear spatial centre and a resolved silhouette.
