# Community conduct

Treat contributors, creators and users with respect. Give specific, actionable criticism of work. Credit sources, disclose relevant limitations, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and sharing private information.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project. Do not post other people's credentials, private correspondence, contact details, or unlicensed media. Use minimal original examples when reporting bugs.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove harmful content and restrict participation where needed. Use GitHub's built-in reporting options for abuse. For concerns requiring privacy, request a safe private contact channel without posting the sensitive details publicly. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
